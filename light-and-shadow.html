On the correspondences used here — Directions follow triplicity (Fire→East, Earth→South, Air→West, Water→North). Candle color, metal, and incense follow the classical planetary rulers of each sign. The 72 Goetic Shadows are paired to the same five-degree zodiac gate as the Shem Angel of matching number, per the old tradition of the Angels holding authority over the Shadows in sequence. Psalm numbers follow Mathers' suggestion of Psalms 1–72 in order. Treat every correspondence here as a starting point for your own gnosis, not a final word — adapt freely to your lineage.

On the Birth Angel triad — Your Birth Angel is your Sun's five-degree Zodiac segment, your Heart Angel follows a separate day-of-year cycle, and your Intelligence Angel is drawn from the exact 20-minute segment of the day you were born (midnight to midnight, not sunrise to sunrise). This follows the Primary/Secondary/Tertiary framework as published by Angelorum. Because that calendar cycle compresses 72 angels into 71-day spans five times a year, a small number of calendar days sit right on the boundary between two angels — on those days this tool resolves in favor of the later one.
